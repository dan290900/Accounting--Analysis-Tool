      About this tool
      
        
      
          This tool implements the AI-assisted annual report analysis workflow:

      
          Step 1 (manual): You paste the NotebookLM KPI extraction.
      
          Steps 2–4 (automated): The tool parses, sends to Claude API, and validates.
      
          Step 5: Copy the final structured output for submission.

      
          Scoring follows the 6-category schema with weighted totals for UC1 (Investor), UC2 (Creditor), and UC3 (Employee).
